What is Prior Authorization?

Prior authorization (PA) is a health-plan process that requires a provider to get approval before delivering certain services, tests, procedures, or medications. Payers use PA to confirm that a request is medically necessary and consistent with clinical guidelines before agreeing to cover the cost. This simulator walks through a simplified version of that workflow: drag the case file between stages, gather documentation, and see how your choices affect the outcome, the timeline, and the efficiency of the case.

✖ Denied
